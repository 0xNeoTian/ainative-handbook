## 写作标准

所有页面遵守同一套标准：

- **先结论，后理由**：每页第一段就告诉你要做什么
- **可操作**：能给命令就给命令，命令都核对过真实存在
- **诚实标注可信度**：✅ 亲历、🧪 试用中、📚 业界做法，没验证过的不写成亲历
- **不泄露隐私**：不出现组织名、客户、同事、服务器和账号信息，每次发布前自动扫描

## English summary

**AI Native Handbook** is a practical guide to building products with Claude Code, written in Chinese by Neo, a founder who, with a few collaborators, runs two organizations largely with Claude Code. It turns 70+ project and incident retrospectives, written over about half a year, into a three-tier path (side tool → product → organization), a comparison of the superpowers and Matt Pocock skills workflow plugins, guardrails, 25 cards that each cite a dated retrospective or work log, reusable templates, and an installable Claude Code plugin. Every page carries a credibility label: lived, trial, or industry practice. An English edition is planned; meanwhile, the full Chinese text is available at [`/llms-full.txt`](https://ainative.jubao.ai/llms-full.txt) for your own AI tools to read and translate.
